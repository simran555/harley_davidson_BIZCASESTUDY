# Harley-Davidson Business Case Study
This case study analyzes the current market position of Harley-Davidson and proposes a strategic investment in hybrid motorcycle technology to address declining sales and expand the customer base.

<b> Project Overview </b>
The project investigates Harley-Davidson as a brand, its current market situation, and its previous venture into electric motorcycles with the LiveWire model. It then introduces a new idea: a hybrid motorcycle design that blends the brand's traditional aesthetics with modern, sustainable technology. The analysis includes a financial feasibility study and a discussion of the benefits of this hybrid approach.

<b> Methodology </b>
The project's methodology is structured to address key aspects of the business and market:

* Harley-Davidson as a Brand: An analysis of the company's legacy, which is centered on the "spirit of freedom and rebellion" , iconic design elements like the teardrop fuel tank , and the "signature rumble of their exhaust".

* LiveWire Analysis: An examination of Harley-Davidson's first all-electric motorcycle , noting its advanced technology and the launch edition price of the S2 Del Mar model at $17,699.

* Market Situation: An overview of declining sales trends, with global registrations at 137,000 in 2023 after nine months, representing a 7.2% decrease and one of the worst performances among the top 20 world manufacturers.

* Proposed Idea - Hybrid Design: The central proposal is a hybrid design that combines an internal combustion engine with an electric propulsion system. This approach aims to open up new market segments while retaining the traditional aesthetic and sound that define the brand.

* Financial Feasibility: A look at Harley-Davidson's 2023 guidance, which projects revenue growth of flat to +3% and an operating income margin of 13.9% to 14.3%. The proposal includes a strategic $74M investment towards hybrid models.

* Benefits of Hybrid Design: The presentation highlights several benefits, including environmental stewardship, market expansion, extended range and efficiency, innovation, and adaptation to regulatory changes.

<b> Key Findings and Business Implications </b>

* Addressing Declining Sales: The presentation identifies that relying solely on an all-electric model while losing old aesthetics can lead to a loss of the traditional customer segment and result in a financial loss.

* Solution with Hybrid Design: The proposed hybrid design, by combining electric power with traditional aesthetics, is presented as a solution to expand the customer segment and increase profit.

* Financial Strategy: A recommendation is made to increase cash reserves to fund the "Hybrid Push" and a proposal to lower the CEO-to-employee pay ratio from 1:539 to 1:10.
